CAREERS AT RENTAMOUT

Help us build a better way to rent.

We’re planning the next stage of rentamout and identifying the people who can help us serve renters and vehicle owners across Nigeria.

Where we are today: rentamout is currently founder-led. The roles below are planned hiring priorities, not confirmation that every position is open or funded right now. We’ll publish application details when each role is ready for recruitment.
OUR PLANNED ROLES

Where we’ll need great people

As the marketplace grows, these are the capabilities we expect to bring into the business. Early hires may begin on a part-time, contract or full-time basis depending on the workload and business needs.

Operations · Priority role

Operations & Customer Experience Associate

Help keep bookings moving smoothly, support renters and vehicle owners, coordinate handovers and follow up on issues so customers get clear, timely help.

Useful strengths: communication, organisation, problem-solving and customer service.
Partnerships · Priority role

Vehicle Partnerships Associate

Find and build relationships with vehicle owners, explain how listing works, help gather vehicle information and support quality onboarding.

Useful strengths: relationship building, sales, field coordination and attention to detail.
Growth & Marketing

Digital Marketing & Content Associate

Help more people discover rentamout through social media, useful content, campaigns, community engagement and performance tracking.

Useful strengths: content creation, copywriting, social media and basic analytics.
Product & Design

UI/UX Product Designer

Make the renter and vehicle-owner journeys easier to understand and use. Turn feedback into wireframes, prototypes and clear product improvements.

Useful strengths: user research, Figma, interaction design and product thinking.
Technology · Quality

Software QA & Testing Associate

Test key flows such as sign-up, vehicle listings, booking and payments, document issues clearly and help ensure updates work reliably across devices.

Useful strengths: structured testing, clear reporting, attention to detail and mobile web testing.
Finance & Administration

Finance & Admin Assistant

Support record-keeping, routine reconciliations, documentation and administrative coordination as transaction and operational volume increases.

Useful strengths: spreadsheets, accuracy, discretion and dependable follow-through.
