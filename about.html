About Rentamout

Take a look under the hood of the company that's making car rental in Nigeria work your way.

The better way to rent, whenever you need a car.

With rentamout, you can rent the exact car you want from local car owners. Explore your options in one place and choose a vehicle that fits the journey you have in mind.

Skip the traditional rental counter and rent on your terms, whether by the hour, by the day, or by the month. Need a chauffeur? Choose one of the cars that offers a chauffeur option.

And if you own a car, rentamout gives you a way to turn the vehicle you already have into income by making it available to people who need a ride.

Company mission

Making car rental simple and flexible for everyone in Nigeria, while helping car owners earn from the cars they already have.
