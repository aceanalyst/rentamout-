THE PEOPLE BEHIND RENTAMOUT

One founder, one big vision, and a team we’re building.

rentamout is currently founder-led. The product, the strategy, the marketing and the day-to-day work are being driven by one person with a clear goal: make car rental simpler and more accessible in Nigeria.

MEET THE FOUNDER

Building rentamout from the ground up.

Founder & Owner

At this stage, the founder wears several hats across the business. That includes shaping the vision, developing the software, deciding what gets built next, introducing rentamout to the market and keeping the business moving.

Rather than presenting a team that does not exist yet, we want to be open about where we are today. rentamout is being built hands-on, one improvement at a time, with the aim of bringing in talented people as the business grows.

What the founder currently handles

  • Business ownership, vision and strategy
  • Software development and product building
  • Marketing, brand and growth
  • Planning, operations and decision-making
  • Working across the customer and vehicle-owner experience
GROWING THE TEAM

The next chapter is built together.

Our future team will bring practical skills, care for customers and a shared belief in making mobility work better. We plan to grow thoughtfully, bringing in people who can take ownership of important parts of the experience.
